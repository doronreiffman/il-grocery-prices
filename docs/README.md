# Design notes

Written while building the project: each note says what was decided and why, the alternatives rejected, and what a review changed. Tags like (q7) are items in the project's work queue, kept outside this repository.

- [collector.md](collector.md): the collector's design: which chains and files, how a morning runs, the write-once archive, identification, alerts and cost.
- [portals.md](portals.md): how each chain's portal serves its files, as observed, and what the code must handle because of it.
- [update-files.md](update-files.md): the update files through the day: the polls, Shufersal's passes, and the update day the next morning's check judges.
- [unreached-names.md](unreached-names.md): a poll that stops on time: the names it had not reached, listed and emailed.
- [operations.md](operations.md): deploying the collector, running it by hand, reading what it wrote and a day of update files, clearing a stopped chain, dropping a chain and what it costs; then building and publishing the shopper site by hand, the headline basket among it, the site run every morning, and deploying step one; then running the dbt layer on the Mac.
- [headline-basket.md](headline-basket.md): the headline basket's design: the basket, the number rules, the pipeline, and the words the page may and may not use.
- [shopper-site.md](shopper-site.md): the shopper site's design: a page per product at nine store formats, the deal against its listed prices, how often it goes on promotion, the front page's deals, the daily forecast and its public record, and what the promotion survey changed.
- [store-first.md](store-first.md): store first, a change to the shopper site: the store picker, then for each product a verdict at the picked store type, "Deal" or "No deal" against what it usually costs there, sales included, with the shekels saved, a price chart, when it was last this low and how often it goes on sale; and a front page of deals per store type. It lists the parts of shopper-site.md it changes.
- [site-look.md](site-look.md): the shopper site's look, "Shelf label": a deal is a green shelf label, a sale that is not a deal a yellow one; a price chart with a ₪ axis, dates and step lines; two typefaces served from the site; one shared stylesheet; every page type, light and dark, phone and desktop.
- [link-preview.md](link-preview.md): the front page's link preview card: the tags an app such as LinkedIn reads, the picture, and what the site check holds them to.
- [dbt-layer.md](dbt-layer.md): the dbt layer: analysis models and data tests over the tables the morning writes, run on the Mac, and why the extraction stays in Python.
- [terms.md](terms.md): the law's clause on using the price files, quoted, and what it asks of the project.
- The build plans, task by task, each with what changed during the build ("Deviations"): [collector-plan.md](collector-plan.md), [headline-basket-plan.md](headline-basket-plan.md) (step one, by hand), [headline-basket-step-two-plan.md](headline-basket-step-two-plan.md) (step two, every morning), [update-files-plan.md](update-files-plan.md) (the update files through the day), [unreached-names-plan.md](unreached-names-plan.md) (a time-stopped poll's unreached names), [shopper-site-plan.md](shopper-site-plan.md) (the shopper site's step one), [store-first-plan.md](store-first-plan.md) (store first: the store picker, the verdict and the price chart), [site-look-plan.md](site-look-plan.md) (the site's look, "Shelf label") and [dbt-layer-plan.md](dbt-layer-plan.md) (the dbt layer).
