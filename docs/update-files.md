# The update files, through the day (design)

Grade: production. The collector is live, and every file it stores is locked for ten years.
Status: draft of 2026-11-06, revised the same day after a builder check (Opus); the owner answered its ten questions the same day, each as the draft recommended (calls 1 to 4, 6, 7, 9 and 13 below), and approved the design the same day. The owner decided on 2026-11-05 that the collector will also collect and keep, write-once, the chains' update files (`Price`, `Promo`) published through the day, for all five chains, Shufersal included. Every choice the owner has not made is marked "Call" and listed at the end. Built and deployed only on the owner's go.

## Why

Each chain publishes two kinds of update file through the day beside the morning's full files: `Price` and `Promo`. Regulation 3(2) of the 2014 price-transparency regulations asks for an update file within an hour of a till change, covering every change since opening (read from a copy in the project's research notes, kept outside this repository; the official text was not read: UNVERIFIED, and the schedule below does not depend on it). Today the collector keeps only the update files its morning runs happen to list: for Rami Levy, Osher Ad, Carrefour and Yochananof those published by about 09:30 to 11:20 Israel time; for Shufersal none (its runs collect `Stores`, `PriceFull` and `PromoFull` only, owner's decision of 2026-11-01). A promotion that starts and ends within a day, or appears only in an update file, is never seen from the full files, and the site's direction is promotions ("Is this deal real?", "Wait for the sale?", "Real deals this week"). The owner also wants every available file kept, used or not.

What the week of 2026-11-01 to 2026-11-06 shows, from the manifests (16 to 19 a day), is what the morning runs saw by the time of each chain's retry run; everything published later is unseen today:

| Chain | `Price` seen by the retry | `Promo` seen by the retry | Rhythm in the names |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rami Levy (by 09:33) | 268 to 275 a day | 82 to 139 | about 90 `Price` an hour from 07:00 (one per store: 99 stores), 50 to 60 `Promo` an hour; a full Saturday (2026-10-31) held 1,206 `Price` and 105 `Promo` ([portals.md](portals.md), "What the directory held") |
| Yochananof (by 10:03) | 139 to 142 | 41 to 94 | 46 or 47 `Price` an hour at 07:00, 08:00 and 09:00 (one per store), 46 `Promo` at 08:00 and 09:00 |
| Osher Ad (by 11:18) | 50 to 60 (21 on 2026-11-02, before its run moved to 08:18) | 6 to 54 | 15 to 24 `Price` an hour at 09:00 to 11:00 (24 stores) |
| Carrefour (by 09:39) | 81 to 242 | 104 to 135 | `Price` at 07:00 to 09:00; most stores' `Promo` at 09:00 (99 to 127 of 147 stores) |
| Shufersal | none collected | none collected | the one record, 2026-11-01's manual runs: 279 of 418 stores' `Promo` listed at 10:14, stamped 09:59:59 (270), 09:40 (2), 03:00 (4) and 02:40 (3); 20 stores' `Price` listed at 09:08, all stamped 09:00 (19 stored, one not attempted when the run stopped) |

One afternoon was recorded: 2026-11-01's manual Rami Levy run at 14:02 (`120241Z-main-4c25`) listed 651 `Price` (5 at 06:00, then 87, 93, 92, 89, 78, 38, 89 and 80 for the hours 07:00 to 14:00) and 407 `Promo` (76, 70, 59, 68, 59 for 10:00 to 14:00). So the hourly rhythm holds through the afternoon, and the morning runs see about a fifth of Rami Levy's day.

Shufersal's update rhythm is stated, not measured: `src/collector/chains.py` (its comment on `CHAINS`) and [operations.md](operations.md), "What runs when", say its update files "come out every hour", from the 2026-11-01 decision; the one record above shows two batches that day, about 03:00 and about 10:00, stamped across stores. Whether a store replaces its file hourly or a few times a day is UNVERIFIED and is what the first week measures (section 7); both places are corrected on build (section 9).

Two portal facts shape the design (both from [portals.md](portals.md)):

- The NCR portal (Rami Levy, Osher Ad, Yochananof) and Carrefour keep the day's files listed through the day and past midnight: at 00:13 on 2026-11-01 the NCR directory still held all of 2026-10-31's 1,946 price and promotion files (the 2,362 entries add up to them, 2026-11-01's first 162, the three store lists and store 039's 251 old-scheme files); Carrefour's page for 2026-10-31 (`?date=`) answered that day's 575 files in the same probe. By 14:02 on 2026-11-01 the previous day's files were gone from NCR's directory (the 1,826 entries then were that day's 1,574, store 039's 251 and one more). So for these four chains a poll sees everything published since the last one, and a lost poll costs nothing as long as a later poll of the day runs. The same directory held 314 `PriceFull` and 321 `PromoFull` for Rami Levy's 99 stores on 2026-10-31: it republishes full files about three times a day (the week's names show batches about 00:00, 06:00 and 12:00), which section 1 keeps out of the polls.
- Shufersal's listing shows only the latest file per store and kind ([README.md](../README.md)), on 22 pages of 20 rows for each kind (page 1's pager named 22 in every run of 2026-11-06 that read it; the probe of 2026-10-31 saw 22 for all four price and promotion categories), and a page took a median 34.7 s (`PriceFull`, 6 pages) and 41.75 s (`PromoFull`, 26 pages) that morning, up to 60.0 s. An update kind's list is in file-name order like a full kind's: 2026-11-01's `Promo` run (`081443Z-main-c96c`) listed its 279 names in that order, no pair out of place, one row per store (checked for this design). A file a store replaces before a run reaches its row is gone for good. So for Shufersal, how often a store replaces its update file decides how much a poll can keep, and no poll can read a kind's list in one run at today's page speeds (section 3).

## Goals

- Every update file a poll lists is stored, byte for byte, write-once, under the Israel date in its name, like every other file.
- Rami Levy, Osher Ad, Carrefour and Yochananof: every update file in the day's listing at the last poll of the day is stored (what they publish after it, and what Shufersal replaces between visits, is the gap this design names and measures, not hides).
- Shufersal: each update kind's list read in full at least once a day, and as often as the host's speed and the cost allow.
- The morning's full files stay the priority: no poll shares a host with a morning run, no Shufersal poll runs before the day's last retry of a full kind has ended, and the morning check keeps its meaning.
- One email a day at most, and only for data lost or a day no poll read, never for a poll that stopped on time or a listing that failed once. (Addendum, 2026-11-08: a poll that stopped on time brings an email only when no later poll stored what it held: data lost; [unreached-names.md](unreached-names.md), ruling R40.)
- The first week measures what is unknown today (each chain's afternoon volume, Shufersal's replacement rhythm, file sizes late in the day) and the owner decides the cadence and the cost from the measurements.

## Non-goals

- No change to the morning runs, their times, or rulings R35, R36 and R37 for the full kinds.
- No reading of a listing sorted by time (Shufersal's header links offer `sort=Time`; it is a new portal call: section 3, fallback).
- No midday full files: a poll takes `Price` and `Promo` only (section 1).
- No per-day sweep of the previous day's files (`day: previous`) in the first build: measured first (section 4, `other_days`).
- No parsing of the files; no thresholds on counts (a chain's day may hold 6 or 1,400 update files).
- No new infrastructure: the same function, bucket, role and alert topic.

## Anti-goals (a failure even if everything else works)

- A poll that shares a host with a morning run, or starts within 15 minutes of one on the same host.
- A poll that makes the morning's full files later or less complete: a block met by a poll latches the chain for every later run (`src/collector/run.py`, `_latch` and the `stopped` check at the start of `collect_chain`), so no Shufersal poll runs between its main runs and the end of its last retry (10:15 to 10:30).
- A check that emails every day: a poll stopped on time, a listing that failed once, a quiet Saturday must stay quiet; and no loss reported twice. (Addendum, 2026-11-08: a poll stopped on time stays quiet unless no later poll stored what it held: data lost, emailed once; [unreached-names.md](unreached-names.md), ruling R40.)
- Storage that grows faster than estimated with no one seeing it: the week's measurement is a gate, not a note.
- More than two polls in flight at once, or any poll in flight while the basket's five extractions run (12:03 to 12:18): the account allows five concurrent Lambda runs in il-central-1, and the flight-board collector must never be starved (section 6).
- A block met by an afternoon poll that the owner learns of a day late, after the next morning's runs did nothing.

## 1. A third collecting mode: `poll`

`{"mode": "poll", "chain": "<slug>", "kinds": [...]}` runs the existing run loop (`src/collector/run.py`) exactly as `main` and `retry` do: read the listing, keep the day's names of the kinds asked for, skip names already stored, download one at a time with the 0.5 s pause, check the body, store write-once, write a manifest under `manifests/date=<day>/<chain>-<HHMMSS>Z-poll-<4 hex>.json`. The day is the Israel date when the run starts, as today; a file belongs to the day in its name.

- A chain gains `polled`, the update kinds its polls read: `("Price", "Promo")` once its poll schedules are deployed, empty before (section 7 deploys the four quick chains first, Shufersal after). `Chain.kinds` keeps its meaning, the kinds the morning collects and the check holds the retries to (rulings R4 and R35): Shufersal's stays `Stores`, `PriceFull`, `PromoFull`. A poll run may ask only for kinds in `polled`; a main or retry run only for kinds in `kinds`, as today. Call.
- Every poll event names its kinds. For Rami Levy, Osher Ad, Carrefour and Yochananof, `["Price", "Promo"]`: the morning runs take all five kinds, and a poll of all five would also store the full files Rami Levy republishes through the day, about two more `PriceFull` and `PromoFull` per store a day at about 872 KB and 230 KB each (medians of the week's stored files), about 200 MB a day for Rami Levy alone, which the owner's decision does not cover. Call: update kinds only; the midday full files are a question of their own (the owner's list). For Shufersal, one kind an invocation, `["Promo"]` or `["Price"]` (categories 3 and 1 in `src/collector/sources/shufersal.py`), as the full kinds run today.
- Idempotency is what it is today: the run reads the day's stored names once (`stored_names`) and never fetches one; a put carries `IfNoneMatch="*"`, so a race with another run stores a body once; a different body under a stored name is kept beside it with twelve hex digits of its hash, but only when a run fetches it, which happens only in a race between two runs ([collector.md](collector.md), "Storage"; `src/collector/store.py`, `put_raw`). An NCR file has a new name each hour (the time is in the name), so the question never arises there. A Shufersal file republished under a stored name is never fetched again and its new body is lost, unrecorded; it needs the same stamp to the second, and Shufersal stamps a batch, not a publication (270 of the 279 `Promo` names of 2026-11-01 carry 09:59:59), so a batch run again with its stamp would do it: how often is UNVERIFIED, and the listing's update-time cell, which the collector does not read, is where it would show. Named here, not handled.
- A poll run never emails, with one exception (Call, section 5): a poll run that latches a chain publishes the `stopped` alert at once, since after an afternoon or evening poll the next check is at 11:48 the next day, after that morning's runs have done nothing. The check's rule R26 applies: the chain is marked alerted only once the publish succeeded, so a lost email is sent again by the check.
- The manifest gains `other_days`: for each Israel date other than the run's, how many names of the kinds asked for the listing carried (today the run skips them without a count). It costs nothing and measures two things the design leaves open: how many of a day's files the next morning's main run still sees (the case for a previous-day sweep), and how much of Shufersal's list is stale. Beside it, a Shufersal poll's manifest carries what every Shufersal run's does: `pages` and, from the q24 build, the list of failed page tries ([shufersal-page-retry.md](shufersal-page-retry.md), section 3).

## 2. The polling schedule

Israel time, as every schedule (`template.yaml`, `ScheduleExpressionTimezone`). Each run has Lambda's 900 s; runs on one host start at least 15 minutes apart (ruling R30); one chain never runs twice at once.

Rami Levy, Yochananof and Osher Ad share `url.publishedprices.co.il`, so a poll of the three takes a 45-minute slot; Carrefour has a host of its own. Every two hours from 12:20, and once more at 23:20 so the day's last poll is as late as the day allows:

| Chain | Poll runs (`{"mode": "poll", "chain": ..., "kinds": ["Price", "Promo"]}`) |
|---|---|
| Rami Levy | 12:20, 14:20, 16:20, 18:20, 20:20, 22:20, 23:20 |
| Yochananof | 12:35, 14:35, 16:35, 18:35, 20:35, 22:35, 23:35 |
| Osher Ad | 12:50, 14:50, 16:50, 18:50, 20:50, 22:50, 23:50 |
| Carrefour | 13:05, 15:05, 17:05, 19:05, 21:05, 23:05 |

Why two hours: Rami Levy publishes about 150 update files an hour (90 `Price`, 50 to 60 `Promo`), and its retry run of 2026-11-01 (`101751Z-retry-851b`) stored 580 files inside one run, so a file costs under 1.4 s with its pause: 300 files in about 5 to 7 minutes, with room for a slow host. The first poll at 12:20 takes what the 09:33 retry did not (about 420 files, 7 to 10 minutes). Three hours would put a 450-file poll near the run's limit on a slow day, and a poll that stops on time leaves its rest to the next one, so a backlog could grow through the day. The directory holds the day, so the number of polls is about spreading downloads and shortening the gap at midnight, not about catching files before they vanish. Osher Ad's last run ends at 00:05: its day is set when it starts (23:50), and names dated the next day are skipped and counted in `other_days`. Osher Ad's afternoon publishing is unseen (its morning files are stamped 07:00 to 11:00); its seven polls cost about a minute each and the first week shows whether they find anything.

Shufersal's passes, each kind two runs 15 minutes apart, the second resuming where the first stopped (section 3):

| Kind | Pass 1 | Pass 2 | Pass 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Promo` | 10:30, 10:45 | 15:10, 15:25 | 20:10, 20:25 |
| `Price` | 11:00, 11:15 | 15:40, 15:55 | 20:40, 20:55 |

Why these: the first pass starts when Shufersal's last full-kind retry (`PromoFull`, 10:15) can have ended, so a block met by a poll can never stop that morning's retries (a latched chain does nothing until the owner clears it), and it ends by 11:30, before the 11:48 check; the batch stamped about 10:00 on 2026-11-01 (272 stores) is taken by 11:30 instead of hours later. The price is the batch before the morning (02:40 and 03:00 on 2026-11-01), which is replaced by about 10:00 before any pass reaches it; a pass before 06:30 would take it, but a block there would latch the chain before the main runs, which is the anti-goal above, so none is scheduled (Call; the owner's list). `Promo` goes first in every pass: promotions are what the site needs. Three passes a day, about five hours apart, are the design's starting cadence, not a measurement: how often a store replaces its update file is unknown, and the first week's records say whether three passes catch each store's files or miss most of them (section 7). Call.

How the polls sit beside the rest of the day: the morning runs (06:30 to 11:33) and the 11:48 check are unchanged, and the check leaves the 10:30 to 11:30 poll manifests out of its morning judgement (section 5); the basket's five extractions start at 12:03 and can run to 12:18, and no poll starts between 11:15 and 12:20; the basket run at 12:33 overlaps Rami Levy's 12:20 poll, two runs of different functions on different hosts. At any moment at most two polls are in flight (a Shufersal run and one of the other four: their windows never overlap each other; Rami Levy's 20:20 sits between Shufersal's 20:10 and 20:25 runs, and the 20:10 run has ended when the 20:25 one starts), and the template tests pin it (section 8). In all, 39 poll runs a day beside today's 17 schedules.

## 3. Shufersal

**Can an update kind be read within a run?** No, not at today's speeds. A kind is 22 pages; at the 2026-11-06 medians a `Price` pass is 22 × 34.7 s = 763 s of page reads and a `Promo` pass 22 × 41.75 s = 919 s, against the 810 s a run has before the 90 s time rule (ruling R32) stops it. Update files are small (about 1 KB) and cheap to fetch: 2026-11-01's `Promo` run listed and stored 279 files in 702 s on a day pages took 15 to 39 s, so its 22 pages took most of the run and a file about 0.6 s with its pause (the 1.2 s a file of [shufersal-page-retry.md](shufersal-page-retry.md) is for full files of hundreds of KB). On a 60 s-a-page morning neither kind gets past page 12 in one run. So an update kind is read the way a full kind is read since ruling R37: across runs.

**What carries over from q21's resume.** All of it, mechanically: a Shufersal poll run records every page it reads (`pages`), and `collector.pages.start_for` and `read_in_full` work per kind from the day's manifests, so `Price` and `Promo` get page records, a resume at the furthest page taken, the one-page overlap for fresh links, the detours to failed files' pages, the step-back when the list shrank, and the order check. The list of an update kind holds one row per store, in file-name order (kind, chain, sub-chain, store, date, time; checked on 2026-11-01's `Promo` run), so a store that publishes a new file changes the name in its row and not the row's place; the order check (`in_order`) holds and the resume page stays right. The names a run `listed` and never stored before it stopped (`not_attempted`) are taken again by the next run from the overlap page, with fresh links. Both functions filter by kind (`_counted`, `_records`), so a poll's `Price` and `Promo` pages never enter a full kind's start point or joining, and the morning's `PriceFull` and `PromoFull` runs never see a poll's pages even when, as now, every poll of the day runs after them.

One thing does not carry over: R37 reads the day as one reading. Once a kind's pages join up from page 1 to the last page, every later run of the day starts a fresh pass at page 1 (`start_for`: "read in full: a fresh pass"), and one page out of order turns joining off for the kind for the rest of the day. An update kind is read several times a day, so the second run of the 15:10 pass would see the 10:30 pass's complete reading and start at page 1 instead of resuming at the page the 15:10 run stopped on, and one odd page at 10:40 would leave every later pass judged as never complete. So, for update kinds only (Call: full kinds keep R37's start rule and `read_in_full` word for word; the passes rule is a function of its own, `passes`, that hands each pass's manifests to the unchanged `read_in_full`, and the morning judgement never sees a poll manifest, so R36's call of `read_in_full` for every kind a retry covers, `Price` and `Promo` included for the quick chains, reads exactly what it reads today):

- A kind's poll runs of the day, in order of `listing_read_at` (not of run id: a run id's time is UTC, and an Israel day begins two or three hours before its UTC day), are cut into passes. A pass runs from its first run to the run whose pages make the pass's own manifests read in full by R37's joining, or to a run that recorded a page of the kind out of order; the next run begins a new pass. A run blocked by the portal is left out, as R37 leaves it out.
- A poll run of a paged kind starts from the current pass: when the last pass is open (not complete, no page out of order), R37's start rule over that pass's manifests alone (a resume at its furthest page, detours to its failed files' pages); otherwise a fresh pass from page 1. So a page out of order costs one pass, not the day, and only a whole pass in one run could complete that pass, as R37 says.

**What carries over from q24's page retry.** A retried page is a page read like any other inside the adapter (`_read_page`, which every kind's listing reaches), so the retry applies to update kinds with no change; a poll's page records carry `tries` and its manifest the list of failed tries, as that design says. Its ruling is R38, so this design's is R39.

**What a pass of two runs gives.** At the 2026-11-06 `PromoFull` median, a `Promo` page with its 12 or 13 files costs about 49 s: the first run reads about 16 pages and stops on time; the second reads page 1, then page 16 again and on to 22, 8 pages in about 7 minutes, and ends with no error: the pass is complete about 22 minutes after it began. On a 60 s-a-page day a page costs about 67 s: the first run reaches about page 12 and the second, with 12 pages to read in 810 s, ends on the last page or one short of it: the pass stays open and the next pass's first run resumes it (at 15:10, hours later; stores that replaced their file in between on the pages already read are not seen until the pass after). The check records each pass's outcome, so the week shows how many passes completed.

**Fallback, if a kind's list cannot be read in full in a day, or stores replace faster than three passes visit them.** In this order, each the owner's call:

1. A third run in each pass (15 minutes more of host time; 6 more runs a day), when the records show passes stopping one run short.
2. A listing read sorted by time: Shufersal's page offers header links `sort=Time&sortdir=ASC` ([portals.md](portals.md), "Paging"); read newest first, a pass would stop at the first page whose names are all older than the pass before, so an idle pass costs one page and a 272-file batch 14. It is a new portal call, unobserved from AWS, with its own risks (ties among files stamped the same second, which Shufersal's batches are, can repeat or skip rows across pages when the sort has no tiebreaker; the name-order check does not apply), so it needs a probe on the owner's go, one request by hand, and a design amendment before any code. Not chosen now (Call): the rhythm is unknown, and the design's first week measures it with no new call.
3. Fewer passes, or Shufersal's update files dropped, under the owner's rule that a chain that causes major trouble is dropped rather than fought.

## 4. What a poll stores and records

- Raw files, manifests, the latch: unchanged (`src/collector/store.py`). A poll's manifest is a run manifest with `mode: "poll"`, `kinds`, `listed`, `files`, `failed`, `not_attempted`, `unparsed`, `blocked`, `error`, `code_sha`, `pages` and the failed page tries (Shufersal) and the new `other_days`.
- How a run knows what is stored: `stored_names` lists `raw/chain=<slug>/date=<day>/` once at the start (one to three listing pages at a few thousand keys a day) and the run never fetches a stored name. Two polls of one chain never run at once (15 minutes apart, 900 s runs), so no two runs race for the same file; a manual run beside a scheduled one would, and the write-once put settles it: one body stored, the other run's manifest says `exists`.
- Nothing is ever stored twice under one key, and nothing is deleted: the same lock, the same role (`s3:PutObject` and `s3:PutObjectRetention` under `raw/` and `manifests/`, `s3:GetObject`, `s3:ListBucket`; no `Delete`).
- A Shufersal poll run reads the day's Shufersal manifests, as its full-kind runs do since R37: one listing and one read per earlier manifest of the day (up to 20 by the evening).

## 5. How the check judges an update-file day

"Complete" cannot mean "every update file the chain published": Shufersal lists only the latest file per store and kind, so a file replaced between two visits was never listed by anyone; the other portals list the day's files, but files published after the day's last poll were listed by no run either. So the check judges what was listed and whether the day was read:

- **The morning, judged at 11:48 of the same day, as today.** The morning judgement reads the day's `main` and `retry` manifests only; the day's poll manifests (Shufersal's 10:30 to 11:30 passes) are left out of `missing`, `listed` and `manifests`, since a pass that stopped on time has listed names it has not yet stored and the afternoon pass takes them. `stored` counts every stored name, so a poll's files never hurt. Rulings R35, R36 and R37 read `main` and `retry` runs and do not change: a poll run never lists a full kind, is not a retry, and is not among the manifests the morning judgement reads, so a poll's block never silences R35's or R37's reasons either (those are dropped only when a retry run was blocked, `src/collector/check.py`, `_unlisted` and `_not_read_in_full`). Call.
- **The update day, judged the next morning.** The 11:48 check of day D also judges D−1, for every chain whose `polled` is not empty and only while the poll switch is on (the function reads it from an environment variable the template sets from `PollScheduleState`, so a switch turned off for a pause emails nothing; Call), from every manifest of D−1, polls included, and D−1's stored names, and writes the result in D's check record under `update_day` (one record, one email a morning; a reader of D−1's update day opens D's record). It emails for two things, and records the rest:
  - `missing`: a name a poll run of D−1 tried and failed (`failed`) that is still not stored, and that no `main` or `retry` run of D−1 listed (those names were judged at D−1's 11:48 and are never reported twice). A name a poll listed and never tried because the run stopped on time (`not_attempted`, `src/collector/run.py`, `_fetch`) is not emailed: on a slow Shufersal evening the day's last run of a kind leaves such names, and the next pass is the next day's; they are counted in the record (`not_tried`, per kind), which is the week's measure of what the time rule costs, and a sweep or a fourth pass is the answer if the count says so. Call. (Addendum, 2026-11-08: a poll that stops on time, of a listing that does not page, now lists the rest of its listing, so its `not_attempted` holds every name of its day it did not reach and the day had not stored before (`src/collector/run.py`, `_drain`). For a kind with no page records the never-tried names are emailed in the `missing` alert, beside the names a poll tried and failed, and `not_tried` holds the names, per kind, leaving out those a `main` or `retry` run of D−1 listed; a paged kind's (Shufersal's, once it polls) stay counted, not emailed, until its polls are designed; [unreached-names.md](unreached-names.md), ruling R40.)
  - `failed`: an update kind in `polled` that no poll run read in full on D−1: for Shufersal, a kind with no completed pass that day; for the others, no poll run that ended with no error and was not blocked (its listing read whole, as R36 reads a run without page records). A whole day of failed polls is a portal change, a bad deploy or a schedule lost, and nothing else tells the owner; the dead-man ping covers only the 11:48 check. Suppressed when a poll run of D−1 was blocked: the `stopped` alert covers it (as R35 does).
  - Quiet, recorded: each poll run's outcome (`polls`: run id, kinds, listed, stored, failed, not attempted, error, blocked); per update kind the completed passes (each the run ids R37's joining names) and the open pass, if any, with what of the list it did not read (the words `unread` already uses); `not_tried`; `by_hour`, the stored update files of D−1 per kind by the hour in their names, which is the week's measurement of each chain's rhythm. A poll stopped on time, a listing that failed and a later poll read, fewer files than yesterday: all quiet. A persistent portal change surfaces the next morning through ruling R4 anyway.
- Ruling: this becomes R39 in [collector-plan.md](collector-plan.md), "Rulings made during the build" (R38 is the q24 page retry), worded by the builder from the bullets above, with the passes rule of section 3.
- The first judged day after a chain's polls are deployed emails "no poll run read ... on D−1" for that chain unless manual poll runs ran that day; the deploy procedure runs one per chain before the switch goes on, as it runs one main run per chain today (section 7).

## 6. Concurrency, Lambda time and cost

## 7. Rollout, each step on the owner's go

1. **Build.** Test-first, the mutation check, a fresh review, as every collector change (`docs/collector.md`, "Testing and shipping"); the docs of section 9 changed in the same branch. 2. **The four quick chains.** Deploy with the four chains' poll schedules, their `polled` set, Shufersal's empty, and the switch off; in the afternoon, one manual poll run per chain, one at a time (the NCR chains never together); read each manifest (`listed`, `stored`, `failed`, `other_days`) and its `REPORT` duration; a manual check the next morning after 11:48 shows the `update_day` section. Then the switch on. Read for three days: the `update_day` sections (no email but for data lost; `by_hour` showing the afternoon rhythm; `not_tried` near zero), the REPORT durations against the table in section 6, and the day's bytes (`aws s3 ls --summarize` on `raw/chain=ramilevy/date=<day>/`, read-only). 3. **Shufersal.** Add its twelve pass runs (section 2), set its `polled`, deploy with the switch on after a manual pass of each kind. Read for seven days: page times for `Price` and `Promo` (`pages`), how many passes completed and what `not_tried` the last pass left (`update_day`), and the hour histogram of each pass's `listed` names (each name's time is a store's latest file: a histogram clustered at a few stamps is a batch rhythm; one spread over the hours is hourly replacement, and three passes then miss most files). 5. **Closes** when seven consecutive days show every quick chain's update day judged with no email but a true loss, each Shufersal update kind read in full at least once on five of seven days, and the costs written down from measurements.

## 8. Tests and mutations

## Risks kept

- Shufersal's replaced files: a store that publishes twice between two visits of its row loses the first file, silently; the hour histograms measure how often, and the fallbacks of section 3 answer it. The batch before the morning (02:40 and 03:00 on 2026-11-01) is lost every day by call 2.
- A Shufersal file republished under a stored name (the same stamp to the second) is lost silently and unrecorded (section 1).
- Names the day's last run of a kind listed and never tried are lost without an email; `not_tried` in the record is the only sign, and the week's read is where it is seen. (Addendum, 2026-11-08: for the four quick chains such names are now listed in full and emailed, though still lost; Shufersal's stay counted, not emailed, until its polls are designed; [unreached-names.md](unreached-names.md), ruling R40.)
- Files published after a chain's last poll of the day (23:05 to 23:50) and before midnight are not collected until a sweep exists; `other_days` in the next morning's main run counts them.
- A lost update file is reported up to 36 hours after it was published; for the NCR chains it may still be in the directory then, and nothing yet re-fetches a past day (the sweep would).
- A block during an afternoon poll stops the chain's next morning too, by the hard rule; the immediate email (call 9) is what gives the owner the evening to clear it.
- Lambda's free tier is shared by three functions in one account, and the expected total sits at its edge; a week of every poll running its full 900 s costs about $2 beyond it, and the $5 budget alarm is the guard.
- The storage estimate's high bound is six times its low bound, from one afternoon's record of one chain; the week's measurement is a gate the owner reads, not a figure to carry forward.
