# The shopper site, step one: implementation plan

Grade: the approved design's (`docs/shopper-site.md`, "Grade"): "portfolio grade, as the headline basket ([headline-basket.md](headline-basket.md)): tested, reproducible, honest in its words; not production. **Goal:** step one of the shopper site: every morning, from the chains' own files, a page per product with its listed prices and promotions at each of the nine columns the site shows (Shufersal's four large store formats, Carrefour's two, and Rami Levy, Osher Ad and Yochananof whole), the promotions' measured gaps, a front page of deals below their recent listed prices, browse pages and search, the method, record and basket pages, and a forecast frozen in public each day and scored 14 days later, with no odds shown to anyone.

**Architecture:** a new package `src/shopper/` beside `basket` and `collector`, stdlib plus the DuckDB already pinned. The site's unit is the **column** (the design's amendment, S1 and "The column"): `shopper.columns` puts each included store in a column each day, Shufersal's by the day's store list's sub-chain, Carrefour's by a hand-kept store table beside `basket.chains.EXCLUDED`, the other three chains whole; everything the site computes (coverage, listed prices, "wide" promotions, the history, the target, the forecasts, the record, the gate) is per column. The scheduled function stays `BasketFunction` in its two modes (design, Call 5): `extract` (12:03, one run per chain) now writes three tables per chain-day under `chain=<slug>/date=<day>/`, prices (as today), promotions and items, the last two keyed by column inside; `basket` (12:33) now builds the whole site: it reads 90 days of items tables, scores the forecasts frozen 14 days back, freezes today's forecasts write-once in the archive, prices the basket (still at chain level), renders every page, runs the site check and the guard, uploads and invalidates. The Mac runs the same code by hand (`python -m shopper.build`), as the basket's hand build does. Pages are plain HTML; `search.js` is the one script.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.13 (`xml.etree.ElementTree.iterparse`, `fractions`, `decimal`, `random`, `concurrent.futures`), `duckdb==1.5.6` (already in `requirements-dev.txt` and the layer), boto3 from the Lambda runtime, SAM and CloudFormation, EventBridge Scheduler (unchanged), pytest and pytest-cov, `scripts/mutation_check.py`; Node 22.11.0 for the tests of `search.js` only (CI step `actions/setup-node@v4`; the Mac has Node 24).
